    Settings
    Keep Dooo on your phone
    Use your browser's menu to install Dooo or add it to your Home Screen. On iPhone, use Share → Add to Home Screen.
    Approval notifications
    Enable notifications to hear when your PC needs you. On iPhone, open Dooo from your Home Screen first.
    PC control
    Your PC must be awake, unlocked, and running Dooo. Clicks, typing and keyboard shortcuts need approval. Scrolling and looking at the screen can continue automatically.
    Press Ctrl + Alt + Esc on your PC to pause control immediately. Resume from the Dooo tray menu.
    Optional brains
    Connected Claude Code and Codex sessions can use Dooo's computer tools when you ask. You don't need either running to control your PC from here.
